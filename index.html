02What Is SHRM?

The biggest name
in people.

SHRM — the Society for Human Resource Management — is the world's largest HR professional association: 340,000+ members shaping better workplaces across 180 countries.

Our chapter is the student arm of that network. Being here plugs you into national resources, scholarships, SHRM-CP certification prep, and a professional community that starts on campus and goes global.
